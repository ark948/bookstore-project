################################################### Naming conventions ###################################################

    <action being done>-<object being affected>-<*type(s)>.html
    - example:
    edit-book-modal-container.html
    new-book-form.html
    books-list.html

    <action>-<object>-<type>

    <action which will be performed - Capitalized><Object being affected - Capitalized><Extra info>Form.py
    - example:
    NewBookForm(forms.ModelForm):
    

- view functions:
    <action being performed>_<object being affected>_<extra info>():

- url routes:
    (AppName is fixed for all routes in current app)
    "/<corresponding view file name>/<action being performed>/"
